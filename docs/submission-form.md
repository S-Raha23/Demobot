## Description (150 words)

Film yourself doing a tabletop task once with your phone. DemoBot watches the video with Muse Spark 1.3, while an open-weight Muse Spark Open gives a second opinion and Muse Wonder settles any disagreement. Muse Voice Transcribe handles narration in English, Hindi and Bengali. SAM 3.1 tracks every object and your hand, and DemoBot measures them in centimetres from four printed markers, even with a handheld camera. Muse Wonder then writes a robot program, tests it on a simulated Franka Panda through tool calls, and debugs it on its own. Next, DemoBot checks itself with the same eyes: SAM 3.1 segments the simulation rendered from your phone's pose, and a Muse Spark Open judge compares it with your real final frame. A swarm of Muse Spark Open agents attacks the program with hard layouts, and Wonder hardens it. You can correct the robot by voice. The result is a verified, robot-agnostic skill. The robot is simulated.

## Meta models used

Muse Spark 1.3 (video), Muse Wonder (agentic coding), SAM 3.1 (video and image), Muse Voice Transcribe, Muse Image, Muse Spark Open (open weights, on Baseten and on Modal).
